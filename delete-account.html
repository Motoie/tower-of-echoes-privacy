Account and data deletion

Tower of Echoes · Agatos Gaming

You can request deletion without installing or reopening the game.

  1. Email rafael.motoie@gmail.com with the subject Tower of Echoes - Account deletion.
  2. Use the email linked to your game account where possible. For a Play Games-only account, include your player name or identifier.
  3. Tell us whether you want to delete your entire account or a particular report or other record. Include the ticket number if relevant.

We may ask for information to verify ownership. Never send passwords or sign-in codes.

What is deleted

A verified account deletion request covers your Tower of Echoes account, associated cloud saves, class progression, game account links and identifiable feedback and bug tickets. Local copies on your device can be removed by clearing the app data or uninstalling. Your Google account and Play Games account are not deleted.

Retention exceptions

Information required for security or legal obligations may be retained; we will explain any applicable exception. Provider backups and logs may expire later under their retention practices. Crashlytics reports are not linked to your game account ID, so an event may not be identifiable using your email alone. See the Privacy Policy for more details.
