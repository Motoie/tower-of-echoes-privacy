Privacy Policy

Tower of Echoes · Agatos Gaming
Last updated: November 6, 2026

Who we are

Tower of Echoes is an independent game by Agatos Gaming, operated by Rafael Ryoiti Motoie. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the game, sign in, sync progress, submit feedback or report a bug. Contact: rafael.motoie@gmail.com.

Account and sign-in information

When you sign in through a supported provider, we receive your account identifier and the profile information made available by that provider, which may include your email, display name and profile image. Supabase manages the game's authentication and session information. Google Play Games features, when available, use your Play Games player identity and profile and may link that identity to your game account. We do not receive your Google password or access your email inbox, contacts or private files.

Progress and settings

We store settings, run checkpoints, deck and equipment choices, class progression, XP and recovery information on your device. When cloud features are used, account-linked run saves, progression and synchronization records are also stored in Supabase so that progress can be restored and synchronized. Authentication credentials are stored using the device's secure storage where supported.

Automatic crash and stability reports

Android release builds use Google Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes, non-fatal errors and app freezes. Reports may contain stack traces, diagnostic logs, timestamps, app version, device model, operating system, memory information, installation identifiers generated by the SDK and recent gameplay context such as class, floor, turn, screen, card or effect. Collection is enabled automatically in release builds and there is currently no in-game switch to disable it. Our Crashlytics integration does not deliberately attach your account email, account ID or full saved game. We do not currently use Google Analytics or advertising identifiers for behavioral advertising.

Bug reports and Alpha feedback

When you send a bug report, we store your description, a ticket number, the game version, device and operating system details and recent gameplay context in Supabase. If you are signed in, the ticket is linked to your account ID and email. Alpha survey submissions include your ratings, comments, account ID, run ID and class. Draft survey answers may remain on your device until you submit them. Please do not include passwords, payment information or other sensitive personal information in free-text fields.

Why we use this information

We use this information to provide sign-in and cloud saves, maintain account progression, prevent duplicate or abusive requests, investigate technical problems, respond to support requests and improve game balance and usability. The current game has no advertising SDK and does not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Service providers and international processing

We use Supabase for authentication, cloud data and submitted reports; Google Firebase Crashlytics for stability diagnostics; and Google services for supported sign-in and Play Games features. These providers process information to operate their services and may process it in countries outside your own. Network requests expose connection information, such as IP addresses, to the service receiving them. If you contact us by email, your message is also processed by our email provider. Privacy policy web hosting may process ordinary connection logs. We do not embed advertising or analytics scripts in this policy page.

Storage, security and retention

We use encrypted network connections and access controls to protect information. No system can guarantee absolute security. Account and cloud progress information is kept while needed to provide the account and its features, or until a verified deletion request is completed. Reports and feedback are kept while needed for support, game improvement and abuse prevention, and are reviewed when you request deletion. Copies in service backups or security logs may take additional time to expire under the provider's retention practices. Google states that Crashlytics retains crash data and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting removal from live and backup systems. Uninstalling the game does not by itself delete cloud records.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can request access, correction or deletion of your Tower of Echoes account and associated data by emailing rafael.motoie@gmail.com with the subject 'Tower of Echoes - Account deletion' or 'Tower of Echoes - Privacy request'. Send the request from the email linked to your game account where possible. For a Play Games-only account, include your player name or identifier so we can help verify ownership. Never send your password. You may also request deletion of an individual report by providing its ticket number. We may need additional information to verify ownership before acting. Account deletion includes associated cloud saves, progression, account links and identifiable reports and feedback, except information that must be retained for security or legal obligations. We will explain any applicable exception. Deleting game data does not delete your Google account or Google Play Games account. Because our Crashlytics reports are not linked to your account ID, we may be unable to identify an individual diagnostic event from your email alone.

Children

Tower of Echoes is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. The game's content rating is declared separately in the store.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the game and its services change. The date above identifies the latest revision. For questions or privacy requests, contact Agatos Gaming at rafael.motoie@gmail.com.
